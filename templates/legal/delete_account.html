You can delete your {{ company_name }} account and your personal data at any time. Choose the option that matches your account.

Option 1: Delete inside the app (fastest)

  1. Customer: log in, open My Account, tap Delete Account, enter your password and confirm.
  2. Vendor: log in to the Vendor Dashboard, scroll to Delete Account, enter your password and confirm.
  3. Rider: log in, open the Kamai (Earnings) tab, tap Delete Account, enter your password and confirm.

If you still have an active order or delivery, finish or cancel it first, then delete.
