{{ company_name }} ("we", "us") runs the {{ company_name }} shopping and delivery app and website (the "Service"). This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, who sees it, and the choices you have. By creating an account you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: name, phone number, email address, city, delivery address and password (stored encrypted/hashed, never in plain text).
  • Order details: items ordered, order status, delivery address, payment method chosen (cash on delivery, etc.), ratings and reviews you give.
  • Vendors (store owners): business name, store description, logo and cover images, products, prices, delivery areas and order history.
  • Riders: name, phone number, vehicle type, online status, delivery history and earnings/settlement records.
  • Messages: chat messages you send to our support team inside the app.
  • Technical data: IP address, device/browser type and basic usage information, used for security, fraud prevention and keeping the Service working.

We do not collect your contacts, SMS, photos or files from your device. We do not use your data to show third-party advertising.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account (including OTP / email verification).
  • To process, deliver and track your orders and to contact you about them.
  • To let vendors prepare orders and riders deliver them.
  • To give customer support, prevent fraud and misuse, and keep records required by law.
  • To send order updates. You can opt out of promotional messages at any time.

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share only what is needed to complete your order:

  • Vendor / store you order from: your name, phone, delivery address and order items.
  • Delivery rider assigned to your order: your name, phone and delivery address.
  • Service providers that help us run the Service (hosting, email delivery), only under confidentiality.
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account we remove your personal data (see section 6). Basic order and accounting records (order number, items, amounts, dates) are kept without your name, phone, email or address, because tax and accounting rules require us to keep business records.

5. Security

Data is sent over HTTPS, passwords are hashed, and access to personal data is limited to people who need it. No online service is 100% secure, so please choose a strong password and do not share it or your OTP codes.

6. Your rights & deleting your account

You can view and update your details in your account. You can also delete your account and personal data yourself at any time:

  • Customers: My Account → Delete Account.
  • Vendors: Vendor Dashboard → Delete Account.
  • Riders: Kamai (Earnings) page → Delete Account.

No access to the app? See how to request deletion. Deleting your account removes your name, phone, email, addresses, chat messages and profile data, and closes your login. This cannot be undone.

7. Children

The Service is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. Continued use of the Service after a change means you accept the updated policy.
